# Source Registry

This registry describes the evidence types an A-share research answer can rely on and what each
one can support. It names no retrieval route: every item is requested through the host data
contract, and the retrieval role decides where it comes from.

## Statutory disclosures and regulators

| Evidence | What it can support |
| --- | --- |
| Exchange disclosure filing (annual, interim, quarterly, board resolution, financing plan) | Reported figures, contracts, financing terms, risk disclosures, governance items |
| Regulator notice or penalty | Rules, enforcement history, filing obligations |
| Enterprise credit registration | Legal entity status, shareholders, administrative filings |

## Ministries, exchanges, associations and public procurement

| Evidence | What it can support |
| --- | --- |
| Industry ministry policy | Industry policy, standards, project eligibility |
| Statistics bureau release | Macro indicators, industry output, regional data |
| Association study | Governance and market-structure context |
| Operator procurement notice | Bid outcomes, package scope, procurement timing |

## Company channels and supplementary public sources

| Evidence | What it can support |
| --- | --- |
| Issuer IR material | Presentations, investor Q&A, event notices |
| Exchange interactive Q&A | Investor questions and management replies, when archived |
| Local government or park notice | Project approvals, land, energy, industrial policy |
| Industry media | Secondary context only, never sole support for a core conclusion |

## Rules

- Core facts, numbers, contracts, financing and governance items need a statutory disclosure or a regulator notice.
- Policy, market size, procurement and regional context can rest on a ministry, statistics or procurement source.
- Company channels and media are supplementary unless they reproduce an official document.
- Every item carries its provenance: venue, document name, document date, and the accession or link the retrieval returned. A source that cannot be recorded this way is not evidence.
- If a source is behind a login or is not publicly reachable, mark it inaccessible and do not use it as the main proof.

## What each question needs

- Company history and business mix: annual report, then prospectus, then issuer material
- Orders and projects: exchange announcement, then procurement notice, then local-government notice
- Debt and financing: annual/interim report, then bond or convertible filing, then board resolution
- Industry policy: ministry policy, then local policy, then association material
- Macro and regional demand: national statistics, then provincial or city statistics, then policy documents
